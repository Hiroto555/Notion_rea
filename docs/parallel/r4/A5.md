## Available change

- Sidebar width is 240px (232/220/208px at existing breakpoints); rows and toolbar controls share a 32px control height.

- Page title size, section spacing and body density are exported as tokens for the record surface. No record-specific geometry or persistence behaviour was changed in this lane.
